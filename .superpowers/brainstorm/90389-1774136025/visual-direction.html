Visual Direction: Low-Res Rendering Styles

All options render at low internal resolution for performance. The difference is in post-processing and color treatment.

A: PS1 Faithful

CRT scanlines + barrel distortion + heavy dithering

The current look, polished. Vertex jitter, affine textures, visible scanlines. Nostalgic and gritty. Colors are muted and warm.

Best for: hardcore retro identity

B: Atmospheric Low-Res

No CRT effects. Rich fog, volumetric light, color grading

Same low internal resolution but without retro filters. Instead, invest in atmospheric depth: layered fog, god rays, biome-specific color palettes. Dreamy and immersive.

Best for: cozy mystery atmosphere, modern indie feel

C: Hybrid -- Subtle Retro + Atmosphere

Light scanlines + fog + color grading + subtle vignette

Keep gentle retro touches (faint scanlines, slight grain, vignette) but pair with atmospheric effects. The retro feel comes from the low resolution and color palette, not heavy filters.

Best for: best of both worlds, distinctive but not harsh

D: Biome-Driven Visual Identity

Each biome gets its own visual treatment

Instead of one global style, each biome has a distinct color grade, fog density, and post-processing flavor. Desert is warm and hazy, swamp is murky and green-shifted, volcanic is high-contrast with red glow. Transitions blend between them.

Best for: making each biome feel like a different world

These aren't mutually exclusive -- D (biome-driven) can combine with any of A/B/C as a base.

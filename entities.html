LORE — The Resonance Builders

An ancient civilization discovered each biome is a frequency. Together they form a chord keeping the world stable. They built Resonance Sites to amplify and connect biomes via Crystal networks. They ascended to Heaven riding harmonics.

The Fall: They tried to create a new frequency — a new biome. The experiment cracked the harmony. Hell is that failed frequency — dissonance bleeding into the world. Creatures feel it. Weather distorts.

The Present: The world is going out of tune. Each Resonance Site can be re-harmonized through ritual. Creatures are the key — they naturally resonate with their home biome. The player pieces together what happened and restores the chord.

Endgame: With 8+ sites activated, enter Hell's center. Stand still for 10 seconds as each harmonic crescendos. Complete the chord. The world remembers its song.
